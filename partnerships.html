Why Partner

See the work before you make the offer.

Traditional hiring is a bet on a resume. A CareerForge partnership lets you evaluate real output first.

For your pipeline

Get a shortlist of candidates who've already trained, simulated the role, and shipped a live project in your domain — evaluated by a mentor before they ever reach your inbox.

For your team

Hand a real (bounded) piece of work to the Build stage instead of a backlog item nobody has time for, and get a fresh, mentored perspective on it — at no cost to bring the brief.

How It Works

Three steps in.

  • 01
    Submit a Brief
    Tell us the track, the scope, and the timeline. We'll confirm fit before it goes live.
  • 02
    Mentors + Participants Deliver
    A mentored cohort works the brief during the Build stage, with regular check-ins from your side if you want them.
  • 03
    You Get First Look
    Review the delivered work and get introduced to the participants who impressed you most — before anyone else does.
